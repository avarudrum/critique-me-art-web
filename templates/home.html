How a critique works here

  1. i

    Hang the work

    Upload the piece and write the question you're actually asking. “Is this finished?” gets you nothing. “Does the background read as a space or as flat colour?” gets you an answer.

  2. ii

    Name your areas

    Composition, colour, technique, concept, perspective. Tick the ones you want looked at. The rest stay closed — nobody is going to lecture you on anatomy when you asked about colour.

  3. iii

    Read it back area by area

    Each critic writes to your areas, one at a time, in their own words. You get notes you can act on instead of a single box with “looks great!” in it.
